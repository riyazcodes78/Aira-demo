AIRA — Client Journey Prototype (Final Concept)

Core pitch:
AIRA manages the brokerage/agent workflow. This concept gives the customer a dedicated place to experience that workflow.

Customer journey:
1. Lead/brief is captured by the brokerage.
2. Agent/AIRA workflow produces matched properties.
3. Customer receives a private property space.
4. Customer can save, compare, view details and book viewings.
5. Customer can see next steps and documents.
6. Agent sees customer intent/actions in the same relationship.
7. The journey continues toward a decision/transaction.

This is intentionally a concept prototype, not a live AIRA integration. Property names, prices, match scores and people are illustrative.
